## 11. On-Page SEO & Content Placement Checklist

To dominate Google ranking for your target keywords:
1. **H1 Tag:** Ensure your homepage has exactly ONE `<h1>` containing the primary keyword:  
   *`<h1>AgentsKaro — #1 Autonomous AI Job Application Bot for Windows</h1>`*
2. **H2 Headings:** Include platform names:
   - `<h2>Autonomous Job Bots for Naukri, Internshala, Indeed & Enterprise Portals</h2>`
   - `<h2>Company Career Crawler with Dynamic Password Studio (v3.1.0)</h2>`
   - `<h2>Beat the ATS: Real-Time Resume Keyword Scoring & AI Cover Letters</h2>`
3. **Internal Linking:** Link phrases like `Naukri auto apply bot` to your features guide or documentation.
4. **Alt Text for Images:**
   - `alt="AgentsKaro AI Job Application Bot Desktop Interface"`
   - `alt="Yubisaki Assistive Technology Logo"`
   - `alt="Company Crawler Dynamic Password Suffix Preview"`
5. **XML Sitemap:** Ensure `sitemap.xml` includes all landing anchors and updates `lastmod` with current date (`2026-09-11`).
6. **Robots.txt:** Allow Googlebot and Bingbot full crawl access with `User-agent: * Allow: /`.
